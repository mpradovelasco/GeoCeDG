`585c536a2986c929b1d64b31ae75967baa9afc9e`. This document is a source-level
requirement/test map; execution results belong to the exact source-bound external
records. It does not assert a GUI gate has passed or adopt a normative proposal.

The R5 PHASE must select every new case and this historical closure. The final
INTEGRATION and FINAL broad selections must discover the new test classes in
their ordinary source sets. A file's existence or a dry-run listing is not an
executed test result. Selection counts and deterministic identity hashes are
derived from the canonical measured producers before the immutable final run.

## Evidence categories and outstanding gates at design time

- R4 kernel/AWT/native-file and audit results remain historical R4 evidence.
- Baseline actual-window failures and intermediate R5 failures remain intact.
- R5 focused tests establish their own source cohort and test boundary.
- GUI-1 to GUI-4 require Computer Use/real OS input; an embedded controller
  assertion cannot mark them PASS. At design time they are **NOT YET VERIFIED**.
- STATIC, INFRA_UNIT, PHASE R5, INTEGRATION and FINAL require the final frozen
  SHA/tree. At design time they are **NOT YET EXECUTED**.
- A successful technical result does not imply author approval, D1 adoption,
  F8 activation, release or CU7 closeout.

For each final GUI record retain launch/source identity, actual window and
operations, screenshots, before/after point and dependent observations, tool
and lock state, rejection messages, save/reopen outputs, and Undo/Redo result.
For each canonical record retain exact command, exit, effective selection,
raw report/receipt and all diagnostic findings, even when acceptance succeeds.
