# CeDG scientific references

This area separates scientific knowledge from executable models. The files
were supplied by the author in the provisional `docs/legacy/` intake and are
retained as local research sources; their presence does not grant publication
or redistribution rights.

- `book/`: the 2023 CeDG book and its methodological corpus.
- `foundations/`: definitions, comparisons, and foundational CeDG evidence.
- `locus-and-intersections/`: LSIM, sampled-locus measurements, and surface
  intersection studies. These are legacy/research evidence, not Locus V2.
- `developments/`: flattening and developable-surface studies.
- `discrete-models/`: discrete-parameter elbows and related model evidence.
- `spatial-association/`: the historical JavaScript/list proof of concept.
  It must not be treated as the production spatial-identity contract.

`catalog.yml` is the checked-in reference and rights inventory.
`public-model-corpus.yml` indexes the public GeoGebra model collection linked
from the book. It is metadata only: remote resources are not build inputs and
no bulk download was performed.
