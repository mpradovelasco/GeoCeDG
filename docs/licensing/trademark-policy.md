# GeoCeDG trademark and brand-use policy

Status: **AUTHOR APPROVED** as project policy on 2026-09-15. The `GeoCeDG`
word-mark application was filed with the OEPM on 2026-09-16; registration is
pending. No grant or completed registration is claimed, and this policy does
not change copyright licenses.

## Covered marks and version-independent identity

The policy covers the word `GeoCeDG` and assets whose manifest record declares
`trademark_role: official-product-mark`, regardless of later pixel, color,
layout, filename, or hash changes. Artwork versions remain separate copyright
works tracked as:

`semantic role -> asset version -> exact hash -> provenance -> copyright class
-> trademark role`.

Replacing official artwork requires a new version/hash/provenance record and
normal visual/package validation, not a redesign of this policy.

## Uses intended to be permitted without specific permission

Subject to truthful presentation, applicable law, and other licenses:

- nominative reference to the GeoCeDG project or unmodified official product;
- accurate compatibility/interoperability statements that do not imply
  sponsorship;
- linking to the official project;
- redistribution of an unmodified, author-approved official package with its
  original notices and branding intact;
- screenshots in scientific, educational, review, news or documentation
  contexts with reasonable attribution; and
- statements that software was forked from, tested with, or compared with
  GeoCeDG, provided the modified product has a distinct name and branding.

## Uses requiring permission or rebranding

- using GeoCeDG as the product or publisher name of a modified fork;
- using an official logo for another product, service, domain, store listing or
  organization in a way likely to imply origin or endorsement;
- presenting a modified logo as official;
- merchandising an official mark;
- removing a distinction needed to avoid confusion; or
- asserting sponsorship, certification or partnership without authorization.

Permission may carry quality-control, attribution or version conditions. This
policy does not predefine commercial trademark-license terms.

## Fork policy

The EUPL-1.2 source grant permits recipients to exercise the software rights it
provides, but the fork must not represent itself as the official GeoCeDG
product. A public modified fork should:

1. choose a distinct product/name and application identity;
2. replace official-product-mark assets and installer/store branding;
3. preserve required copyright/license notices and describe modifications;
4. use GeoCeDG only nominatively, for example “based on GeoCeDG”; and
5. avoid confusing domains, package identifiers and publisher fields.

This separation makes source-code freedom compatible with product-origin and
quality-control signaling.

## Copyright and trademark separation

CC BY 4.0 may authorize copying and adapting an artwork file, but it does not
by itself authorize trademark use that suggests official origin. Conversely,
trademark permission does not grant source files or copyright adaptation
rights. The manifest records both dimensions.

Application, registration and enforcement are separate states. The historical
`D1-HD-03` disposition correctly deferred filing on 2026-09-15; a later author
action superseded that operational state by filing the Spanish denominative
mark `GeoCeDG`, application `M4403138`, on `2026-09-16 12:09:08`, Nice class
`09`. The authoritative filing evidence is indexed in
[`trademark-registration/README.md`](trademark-registration/README.md).

That application remains pending. It is not a registration identifier or
evidence of grant, and no registered-trademark symbol may be used. No
versioned evidence establishes an application for a figurative mark. Current
logo/icon/splash assets remain independently tracked copyright works and
official-product branding; any later figurative filing must identify its own
application, representation and evidence.
