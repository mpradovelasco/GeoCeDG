# GeoCeDG PROFILE COMMERCIAL

Status: **NOT YET AUTHORIZED FOR COMMERCIAL DISTRIBUTION**.

This document is a compact policy and navigation index. It does not grant
rights, record third-party consent, or replace the D1 evidence.

## COMMERCIAL-A — preferred short-term route

The preferred route retains the current architecture and seeks a GeoGebra
License and Collaboration Agreement for the retained GeoGebra Language Files,
UI images/styles and other applicable Materials. GeoGebra-authored source code
made available under EUPL-1.2 remains independently governed by that grant;
the commercial agreement is needed for the separate Materials boundary, not
merely because the code originated upstream.

OpenGeoProver requires a separate commercial clarification or permission. Its
archived GPLv3 project metadata and source-level non-commercial banner support
the approved NC disposition but are not interpreted permissively for
commercial use. GeoGebra cannot grant that independent permission on behalf of
the OpenGeoProver rightsholders.

The remaining audited third-party set—including Giac/GMP/MPFR,
math-cross-platform, renderer code/fonts, Temurin, JOGL/GlueGen/JNA and the WiX
payload—is commercially compatible under the recorded individual grants and
obligations. This does not eliminate notices, reciprocal-source duties or the
need to re-audit a changed composition.

No contact has been made in this task. Prepared, unsent templates are:

- [GeoGebra commercial contact](templates/geogebra_commercial_contact.md)
- [OpenGeoProver commercial contact](templates/opengeoprover_commercial_contact.md)

## COMMERCIAL-B — future independent-material route

COMMERCIAL-B remains a future strategy: retain eligible EUPL code while
replacing GeoGebra Language Files, UI images/icons/styles, other NC Materials
and non-nominative upstream marks with GeoCeDG-owned or otherwise compatible
resources. It is a substantial localization, accessibility, UI, packaging and
maintenance program. No implementation is authorized by D1 closeout.

## Branding does not supply commercial permissions

The `GeoCeDG` denominative mark has an OEPM application filed in Spain,
application `M4403138`, and registration is pending. There is no versioned
evidence of a figurative-mark application. The filing does not replace or
satisfy the pending GeoGebra or OpenGeoProver permissions and does not
authorize use of a registered-trademark symbol.

Filing facts are controlled by the
[trademark-registration evidence](trademark-registration/README.md).

## Decisions still required

- authorization to contact and negotiate with GeoGebra;
- acceptance of GeoGebra's eventual terms for the exact retained Materials;
- authorization to contact OpenGeoProver rightsholders and acceptance of an
  explicit commercial clarification or permission;
- a later author decision if COMMERCIAL-B should become a funded product
  program; and
- a fresh closure review for any changed commercial package composition.
